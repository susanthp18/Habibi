## Sidebar placement

**CRM & resolution**, after "Upsell & leads". It's the group whose job is deciding what to do about a specific borrower's account — Promise to pay, Disputes, Callbacks, Upsell are all "what happens next on this account". Decision intelligence answers exactly that question, one layer up. It's not Compliance & QA (that group audits what already happened) and not Bot configuration (it configures nothing).
