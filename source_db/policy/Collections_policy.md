# HDFC Bank — Retail Collections Policy (voice agent corpus)

## Late fee

Late payment fees apply when the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date
printed on the statement. The late fee amount is disclosed on the monthly
statement and may vary by product. Collections agents must not invent or waive
late fees on the call — escalate waiver requests to a supervisor.

## Grace period

A short grace period may apply after the due date for some products before a
late fee is posted. Grace-period eligibility is account-specific. If the
customer asks whether they are still in grace, verify from the CRM position
and do not guess.

## EMI bounce / failed auto-debit

When an EMI or auto-debit fails (insufficient funds, closed account, mandate
issue), the installment remains outstanding and bounce charges may apply as
per the product schedule. Offer to schedule a promise-to-pay or a callback for
mandate update — do not promise charge reversal on the call.

## Restructuring

Loan restructuring or settlement requires a documented review. Voice agents
may log interest and hand off to a specialist; they must not quote settlement
percentages or approve restructuring live.

## Hardship

If the customer discloses financial hardship (job loss, medical emergency),
acknowledge empathetically, offer a callback for hardship review, and escalate
when distress is severe. Do not pressure for immediate full payment.

## NOC / account closure

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) or account-closure letter is issued only after
the outstanding balance is cleared and internal checks complete. Agents must
not promise same-day NOC issuance.

## Recording and identity

All collections calls are recorded for quality and compliance. Account details
are shared only after identity verification (registered mobile last-4 or
account tail).
