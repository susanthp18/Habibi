Q: What happens if I miss my payment due date?
A: A late fee may be charged if the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date on your statement. The exact fee is shown on your statement. Please arrange payment or a promise-to-pay as soon as you can.

Q: Is there a grace period after the due date?
A: Some products allow a short grace period before a late fee is posted. Whether your account is still in grace is account-specific — we can confirm from your account position after verification.

Q: My EMI auto-debit bounced. What should I do?
A: The installment stays outstanding and bounce charges may apply per your product schedule. You can set a promise-to-pay date or request a callback to update your mandate. We cannot reverse bounce charges on this call.

Q: Can you waive the late fee?
A: Late-fee waivers need supervisor review. I can log your request and arrange a specialist callback — I cannot approve a waiver on this call.

Q: Can I restructure my loan or settle for less?
A: Restructuring and settlement need a documented review. I can note your interest and escalate to a specialist. I cannot quote settlement percentages or approve restructuring live.

Q: I lost my job / I am in financial hardship. What are my options?
A: I am sorry you are going through this. We can schedule a hardship-review callback so a specialist can discuss options. If you prefer, I can also connect you to a human agent now.

Q: When will I get an NOC after clearing my dues?
A: An NOC or account-closure letter is issued only after the outstanding balance is cleared and internal checks finish. Same-day issuance is not guaranteed.

Q: Why do you need my mobile last 4 digits?
A: We verify identity before sharing any account details so your information stays private. Please share the last 4 digits of your registered mobile.

Q: Is this call recorded?
A: Yes. Collections calls are recorded for quality and compliance.
