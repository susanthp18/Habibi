HDFC Retail Collections — caller benefits summary (agent reference)

- Clear dues by the statement due date to avoid late fees where applicable.
- Set a promise-to-pay with a concrete date when you need a few more days.
- Request a hardship-review callback if you face job loss or medical emergency.
- Update auto-debit mandates after an EMI bounce to reduce repeat failures.
- After full clearance, request NOC / account-closure documentation through
  the normal fulfilment process (not same-day guaranteed on the call).
- Speak only to the verified account holder; third parties cannot receive
  balance details.
