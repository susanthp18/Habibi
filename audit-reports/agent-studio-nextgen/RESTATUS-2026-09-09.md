## Still open, hardest consequence first

1. **SHIP-05 — a rollback that does not roll back.** `rollback_bot_deployment`
   never touches `deployment_experiments`, and `pick_deployment_id` returns the
   canary deployment id without checking it is still active. An operator who
   rolls back a bad deployment keeps sending the experiment's share of traffic to
   the version they just rolled back, and the fleet screen shows the rollback as
   done. Two candidate fixes, both small: retire the running experiment inside
   the rollback transaction, or have `pick_deployment_id` fall through when the
   canary row is not `active`. The second also covers every other way a
   deployment can stop being live.
2. **CONNECTORS-5 — Bind does nothing on its own.** `ext.*` names reach the Grant
   but are stripped from every idle offer, and re-added only for tools named by
   the *active* skill pack. Binding a connector to a card, with no skill listing
   its tools, changes nothing any model can call. Either the strip is wrong or
   the Bind control is describing a capability it does not deliver.
3. **CONNECTORS-6 — an unremovable binding.** The Connectors tab renders only
   approved connectors, and the Unbind control lives inside that list, so a
   binding to a since-de-approved connector cannot be removed from the UI.
4. **EVALS-2 — the requirement and its runner are on different screens.**
   `EvalRequire` admits `"twin"` and G11 blocks on it, reading `twin_runs`. The
   Twin runs from the Sandbox inspector, not the Evals tab where the requirement
   is set, and neither the gate's detail nor the checkbox says so.
5. **EVALS-3 — `"capability"` gates nothing.** Offered as a publish requirement,
   read by no gate. Connect or delete.
