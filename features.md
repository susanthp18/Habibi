# Collections Agent - Inbound: Bot Functionalities

1. **Caller identification** – match WhatsApp number to account, preload context
2. **Identity verification (fallback)** – for shared/unmatched numbers
3. **Dues & balance lookup**
4. **Payment history check**
5. **Payment method guidance** (informational)
6. **EMI/repayment schedule explanation**
7. **Late fee & waiver policy info**
8. **Dispute capture** (flag for human review, not resolve)
9. **Policy/FAQ answering** (RAG-grounded)
10. **Document/statement request handling**
11. **Contextual upsell offer** (eligibility-checked, sentiment-gated)
12. **Upsell interest capture**
13. **Compliance disclosures** (recording notice, prohibited-language guardrail)
14. **Sentiment monitoring & escalation trigger**
15. **Human handoff with context**
16. **Call summary generation**
17. **CRM writeback**
18. **AHT & upsell conversion logging**
